I am a Lecturer in Machine Learning at Queen's University Belfast, where I direct the QUB Machine Learning Lab. My research concerns the imperfections machine learning meets in reality. For several years I worked at the model-construction stage, on learning from supervision that is noisy, coarse, or drawn from outside the label space. As foundation models have made intervention at that stage progressively impractical—most deployed models are called rather than built—my focus has moved to the inference stage: how to quantify the risk of a given prediction, and what can still be guaranteed when the evidence used to assess it is itself imperfect.

Prior to joining QUB, I was a Research Fellow in the IIML Group at UCL, collaborating with Prof. Miguel Rodrigues. I received my Ph.D. from QMUL, supervised by Prof. Ioannis Patras and Dr. Georgios Tzimiropoulos, and spent time as an AI4Media Junior Exchange Fellow at the University of Trento with Prof. Nicu Sebe. In summer 2026 I was a Visiting Researcher at the KUIS AI Center, Koç University. I hold a Master's from Tsinghua University (supervised by Prof. Jin Gu), and a Bachelor's from Nankai University.

On a personal note, I grew up in Chizhou, Anhui Province, China—a beautiful small city located near the renowned scenic area of Huangshan (Yellow Mountain).
